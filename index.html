Typed answers: write just the name of the concept. Capitals, hyphens, plurals and words like “the”, “principle” or “assumption” are ignored, so Going-concern, going concern and the going concern assumption all count. Spelling does count. For numbers, type the digits: 12500, 12,500 and €12,500 all work. Account names work too (accounts receivable, receivables).
